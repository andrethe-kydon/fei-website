Why This Matters

Every business is leaking hours. Most people cannot see where.

Think about the work that quietly eats a week. The quotation that takes three days because someone has to dig out the last five like it. The enquiry that arrives on Friday evening and gets answered on Monday afternoon. The report rebuilt by hand every month. The answer that only one person in the company actually knows, so everything stops when they are on leave.

Everyone can feel that this is wasteful. Very few people can look at it and say precisely which parts a machine should carry, which parts still need a human decision, and how to build the thing that closes the gap. That is the skill this series teaches, and it is worth considerably more than knowing how to prompt a chatbot.

01

You learn to see the work

How to take a real process apart, follow where the hours actually go, and separate the steps that need judgement from the steps that only need doing.

02

You learn to decide what a machine should hold

Which model suits which job, what your business knows that the AI needs access to, and where to put an approval gate so nothing reaches a customer unchecked. Knowing what to keep human is as much of the skill as knowing what to automate.

03

You learn to build it

Agents that work from your business knowledge rather than generic guesswork. Workflows that run a process end to end. Systems that keep running when you are not watching.

04

You learn to prove it holds

Stress test it, cost it, and know how it fails before it fails on a client.

Put those together and you are not someone who uses AI at work. You are the person who is called in when a team is drowning in manual work, who can diagnose the problem, design the system, build it, and show that it runs. Inside a company, that is the person who becomes indispensable. On your own, that is what an AI consultant does, and it is work businesses are actively paying for.

Questions, Answered

What people ask before they enrol

Do I need prior experience or my own business to join?
No. There are no prerequisites beyond basic computer literacy and a laptop. Participants without their own venture build on Brightleaf Bookkeeping, a fully prepared reference business supplied in every course starter pack, so every exercise runs on real material from day one.
Are the courses SkillsFuture claimable?
Not yet. Future Edge Institute is working on obtaining its licence as a Registered Training Provider from SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG). AOP courses are currently premium commercial programmes. You can register interest to be notified the moment funding support becomes available.
What certificate do I receive?
A Certificate of Completion issued by Future Edge Institute, awarded on meeting all deliverable gates and passing every declared assessment for the course. Assessments are practical: live builds, projects and panel interviews, never multiple choice.
What do I actually leave with?
Working assets, not notes. Depending on the course: tested AI agents, a live knowledge base, running n8n automations, a priced offer with a real pipeline, or a launch ready business, all built and assessed live during the course.
How are classes delivered?
Full day, in person, hands on sessions of 8 hours (6.5 taught plus breaks), in small cohorts. Courses run on consecutive days or one day per week without any change to content.
Do I need to buy any software?
No. Courses run on free tier accounts of the platforms taught, such as Claude, Notion and n8n, set up before Day 1 via joining instructions. You need your own laptop.
Can my company book a private run?
Yes. Any course can run privately for your team, at your office or our venue, and courses can be combined into capability pathways or scoped as a custom programme after a needs discovery session. See For Corporates.
Where do these courses come from?
They are extracted from Kydon's flagship five month AI entrepreneurship programme, the One Person Company Launchpad, rebuilt as six standalone professional courses with the same build first method and assessment gates. The fourteen year story behind the method is on our about page.
