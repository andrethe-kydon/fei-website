Analytics and advertising cookies are set when you arrive on this site. You can stop them at any time, and you do not need our help to do it: every current browser lets you block or delete cookies for a single site, private browsing windows discard them when you close the window, and Google publishes an opt out extension for Google Analytics. Blocking or deleting these cookies does not affect your ability to browse this site or submit an enquiry, and nothing on this site is withheld from you for declining them.

If we cancel or reschedule

We run small cohorts deliberately, and every course needs a minimum number of confirmed participants to go ahead. We will confirm to you in writing that your course is running at least 10 days before the start date. If we cancel or reschedule, you choose: a full refund of everything you have paid, or a free transfer to the next available intake.

Please bear this in mind before booking travel or accommodation that cannot be refunded, as we cannot reimburse costs you arrange independently.

If a session is disrupted by something outside our control, we will reschedule the affected hours so that you receive the full course duration.

Attendance, assessment and certification

A Certificate of Completion is issued by Future Edge Institute where you have:

  • Attended at least 75 percent of scheduled course hours
  • Met every deliverable gate for the course
  • Passed each declared assessment at the 70 percent threshold

If you do not pass an assessment, you are entitled to one reassessment at no charge, to be taken within 30 days. Where you need longer, or a further attempt, we will discuss the arrangement and any fee with you before proceeding.

Missing a scheduled day does not remove the attendance requirement. Where a later intake is running, we will offer you the equivalent day on that intake so you can meet it.
